# Built-in callouts

Every call is assembled when dispatch offers it: the situation below, plus a location, a suspect
and how each suspect will react (cooperate, run or fight). The same call can play out very
differently twice. Dispatch paces the shift: after a big call the next one is calmer and comes a
little later, a long quiet spell makes something bigger more likely, and recent calls, places and
suspects are less likely to come straight back. Plugins can add their own (see the [plugin guide](PLUGINS.md)).

## On foot

| Call | What happens |
|---|---|
| Suspicious person | Someone acting strangely near a store or public place. Usually harmless, sometimes not. |
| Shoplifting | A shoplifter left a store on foot. May give up, run, or turn violent. |
| Disturbance | A rowdy or drunk person shouting at passers-by. Calm them down or take them in. |
| Person with a weapon | Someone seen carrying a knife or bat in public. Approach with caution. |
| Armed robbery | One or two robbers with handguns at a store. They may surrender, run or shoot. |

## In vehicles

Dispatch tracks the vehicle for you. Turn on your emergency lights behind it: a cooperative driver
pulls over, anyone else flees and the call becomes a pursuit.

| Call | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stolen vehicle | A stolen car with one or two occupants. Some pull over, many run. |
| Possible drunk driver | A driver swerving across lanes. Pull them over and test them, if they stop. |
| Shots fired from a vehicle | Two or three armed gang members in a car. Expect a chase and gunfire. |
