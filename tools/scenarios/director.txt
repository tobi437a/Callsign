# a call that was taken and then called off does add some. Ends off duty.
mark scenario director
duty on
wait 300
expect director=[tension=0.00

decline
expect director=[tension=0.00
call suspicious-person
expect recent=suspicious-person,shoplifting
accept
wait 500
cancel
wait 300
expect! director=[tension=0.00
duty off
